# Bit-Burbs business economy correction — 17 November 2026

Baseline: GitHub main 4f5a67e38cb4a5dd69823341c481c174a099d78f.

## Why earnings felt low

Old batches lasted 30–180 minutes, deliveries took 5–60 minutes, and after ten minutes without interaction the visible game paid the same one-third rate as offline play. Higher-end level-one business purchase payback reached 148–171 operating hours even before housing/utility investment. Starter restocking was frequent and most stocked businesses expired early in a long absence.

## Released correction

- Business income multipliers over the last release: 2× below 100,000 purchase coins; 2.25× below 500,000; 2.5× above that. Applies at every upgrade level and still scales with actual employees.
- Stock runs: 90 minutes, 2 hours, 3 hours or 4 hours depending on the old batch tier. Deliveries: 3, 5, 12 or 20 minutes.
- Restock fees use 4% of the former batch duration at the new rate. Across all 115 business/level combinations the upfront fee is no higher than the old fee, apart from at most one coin of rounding. Longer stock does not mean a larger upfront bill.
- Visible idle play earns full business coins while stocked. Only recent interaction counts for operation XP and daily activity. Offline/hidden play earns 50% while stock lasts. No automated offline reordering.
- HUD coins/min includes active boosts.
- Before changing existing batches, credit unsettled old time with the old rates, durations and one-third offline factor. Then preserve each remaining stock fraction, existing delivery timestamp, money, buildings, level, XP and inventory. The migration receipt makes this one-time.
- Housing, purchase/upgrade prices, fixed jobs, resident arrivals, XP curve, BinLink fares, three free car parks and 10% demolition refunds stay as previously shipped. No crate/wheel/shop reward changes, Diamond removal or pending mate compensation in this release.

## Progression and rewards checkpoint

Level 100 still requires 121,171 XP. The prior two-hour daily budget (720 operation + 220 daily-task + up to 100 development + 200 average weekly XP) gives about 97.7 days. This is a budget calculation; actual play, missed tasks, gates and additional XP sources affect real timing. Passive/away coin improvements do not award extra operation XP.

The approved future crate tables are not implemented by this patch. Their direct coin expected values per voucher are Silver 506.25, Gold 583.33, Emerald 428.57, Ruby 383.33 and Diamond 346.875. Silver also currently offers the best direct deeds and dosh per voucher; higher tiers trade efficiency for larger individual rewards and Diamond-only buildings. Do not silently change approved tables.

Future voucher target: 98/week from all ten tasks, plus 1.1 per wheel spin including the every-ten-spin milestone. One daily free spin plus two activity-earned spins/day gives 121.1/week; two daily free claims plus two earned gives 128.8. Three weekly free shop slots, if chosen uniformly from the five approved free offers, add 0.6 expected wheel spins or 0.66 crate vouchers/week. These are assumptions for the planned release, not current live rewards.

The old rent-collection wheel-ticket bar is not a reliable source in the revised business economy because new rent is not being generated by that ledger. Replace it in the wheel release with one ticket per 60 active business minutes, capped at two activity-earned tickets per Brisbane day, while keeping banked/crate tickets spendable. This source remains proposed and should be reconciled with old weekly/level ticket grants so they are not double-counted.

Crate→spin→voucher feedback remains below 2.2% of vouchers spent in the most favourable direct-spin case (Silver: 0.04 spins / 2 vouchers × 1.1 vouchers/spin). Dosh→shop→wheel→crate paths cannot manufacture net dosh in expectation at the proposed prices: wheel direct dosh EV is 2.25/spin, and even spending every returned voucher on the highest direct-dosh-efficiency Silver only adds 2.0625 dosh per spin on average before the small repeating loop. Bought shop vouchers are extra expenditure, not included in the 110–130 normal earned weekly target. Final task accessibility, legacy reward conversion and item storage remain prerequisites for the later crate release.
